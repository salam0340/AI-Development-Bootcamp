## 🚀 About the Bootcamp

Welcome to the **AI Development Bootcamp**, a complete hands-on journey designed to help you go from the **basics of AI and machine learning** to deploying **real-world applications** using Python and modern frameworks.

---

## 💡 Idea For

This bootcamp is crafted for:
- 💻 Beginners looking to break into AI
- 🎓 Students and educators seeking structured learning
- 👨‍💼 Professionals aiming to apply AI to real-world challenges
- 👩‍🔬 Enthusiasts passionate about machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and AI product development

---

## 🧩 What You’ll Learn

- 📚 AI & ML Fundamentals
- 🔍 Supervised & Unsupervised Learning
- 🧠 Deep Learning with TensorFlow & PyTorch
- 🗣️ Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- 👁️ Computer Vision & CNNs
- 🛠️ Model Evaluation & Tuning
- 🚀 End-to-End AI Projects
- ☁️ Deployment with Flask, FastAPI, Streamlit
